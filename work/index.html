Research analytics

The analysis behind the bench work: assay modelling, expression data and structure-based pharmacology, built as tools other people can use.

Luminex analysis dashboardLive

A multiplex biomarker screen in postmortem brain (TTUHSC). The original straight-line fit never matched the plate software; a weighted 4PL curve plus the software's hidden 2× sample dilution now reproduces its concentrations within ±20% for 90–100% of samples on most analytes. Methods only; the study is unpublished.

4PL / 5PL fitting · assay QC · normality testing · R · FastAPI · React
Nicotine · four brain genesLive

The qPCR data behind the 2023 Frontiers in Psychiatry paper, plotted per subject: CERKL, CHRNA10, SMYD1 and FA2H in postmortem smokers and non-smokers, and in nicotine-treated rats split by sex and diet. In female rats, CERKL moves the way it does in human smokers; in males on a high-fat diet it goes the other way.

qPCR · cross-species design · sex × diet · R · SVG
qPCR atlas · vicarious defeat stressLive

Exploratory qPCR from a vicarious defeat stress study, unpublished and shown with the lab's agreement: 13 targets across 7 plates, GAPDH-normalized, with the fold-change heatmap and per-gene distributions. The QC tab keeps the problems visible — two standard curves came back inverted, with the more dilute template reading a lower CT than the neat one, a 4.3-cycle paradox on MOBP. Both are refitted, the raw values suppressed, and one control flagged for roughly threefold lower RNA input.

qPCR · ΔΔCt · standard-curve QC · React
Psilocin · 5-HT2ALive

Docking psilocin, serotonin and related tryptamines against the 5-HT2A receptor, scoped to a single consumer GPU. The protocol reproduces the crystal pose to 0.780 Å, then ranks the prodrug above the active metabolite by 0.9 kcal/mol — five times the seed noise, and stable with side-chain flexibility. The page shows the poses, what that likely reflects, and the explicit-solvent run that would test it.

AutoDock Vina · GROMACS · MD
SERT S438T · escitalopramLive

How a serotonin-transporter variant changes antidepressant binding. Five-seed docking in rigid and flexible receptors predicts the experimental direction: escitalopram binds S438T more weakly. The size is about thirteen times smaller than measured and the two poses nearly coincide, which points at the binding pathway, the question molecular dynamics is staged to answer. Shows the poses and the per-seed spread.
