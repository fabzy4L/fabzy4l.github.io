A number smaller than its own noise

SERT S438T · escitalopram · AutoDock Vina 1.2.5

The S438T substitution in the serotonin transporter weakens escitalopram binding about 320-fold in the lab. This asks a narrower question: how much of that does rigid docking actually see? The first answer was wrong, and the way it was wrong is the useful part.

01

The result that was published, and withdrawn

The first run used one seed at exhaustiveness 8 and returned ΔΔG = −0.2 kcal/mol: the mutant binding more tightly than wild-type. That is the opposite of what the experiment shows, and it sat in the public README until 27 October 2026.

It was not a coding error. The difference it reported was smaller than the search noise of the run that produced it, so its sign carried no information. A single docking run returns a number whether or not that number means anything.

02

What five seeds show

Re-run across five seeds at exhaustiveness 32, in both a rigid receptor and one with flexible binding-site side chains. Each circle is one seed; the bar is the mean.

Seeds 42 · 123 · 456 · 789 · 1001 · hover a point for its value · box 25 Å cubed

The two clusters do not overlap, in either model. In the rigid run, wild-type spans −8.596 to −8.563 and the mutant −8.322 to −8.290, with nothing in between. Seed-to-seed spread there is 0.013 to 0.015 kcal/mol; the gap between the groups is about twenty times that.

Both models now agree on the direction, and they agree with each other to within 0.06 kcal/mol. The sign is stable; that is all the sign is being asked to do.

04

What it still cannot tell you

The experimental 320-fold Ki increase (Andersen et al., 2009) corresponds to roughly +3.4 kcal/mol. Docking returns +0.27. The direction is right and the magnitude is off by about thirteen times.

That gap is the expected behaviour of the method, not a bug in this run. Rigid docking scores a static bound pose; it does not model the desolvation penalty or the disrupted Na⁺ coordination that S438T is thought to cause, and those act on the pathway into the site rather than on the final pose. Molecular dynamics is the next step, and until it runs there is no magnitude here worth quoting.

Vina's own scoring function carries roughly ±0.5 kcal/mol of uncertainty — larger than the 0.269 measured here. Five seeds establish that the ranking is reproducible within this method. They do not make a 0.27 kcal/mol difference physically meaningful on its own.
